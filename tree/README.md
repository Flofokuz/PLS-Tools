# 🌳 T.R.E.E.
### Tactical Recovery Entrepreneur Engine

**Type it in. Watch it recalculate.**

T.R.E.E. is a break-even and recovery calculator for event tabletop entrepreneurs, vendors, and independent professionals. It shows you what an event really costs, how much of that the organizer actually covers, and exactly how many sales it takes to walk away in the green, before, during, and after the event.

## 💡 Why T.R.E.E. Exists

Too many vendors pay the table fee, work the whole day, and still don't know if they made money. Organizers promise foot traffic and funding that doesn't always show up. T.R.E.E. turns that guesswork into a plan you can track in real time.

---

## 🧰 The Six Modules

**01 · Booth Cost & the Funding Gap**
Enter your table fee, inventory, travel and lodging, staff/your time, and print/signage. Subtract what the organizer promised to contribute, and T.R.E.E. shows your **net exposure**, the gap you have to recover. A donut chart breaks down where your money went.

**02 · On-Site Break-Even**
Enter your average sale price and the organizer's promised foot traffic. You get the **units needed to close the gap** and the **sell-through rate** required. The gauge turns green, gold, or red depending on how realistic that number is.

**03 · Live Day-of Tracker** *(optional)*
Check in during the event: time elapsed, actual foot traffic, units sold so far. T.R.E.E. tells you if you're **on pace** or **behind, push marketing** while there's still time to act.

**04 · Follow-Up Recovery** *(optional)*
Leads you capture at the table turn into sales later, if you follow up. Enter your leads and expected conversion rate to see the **revenue you can still recover after the event**.

**05 · Network Tap-In** *(optional)*
Models leads who join a membership or network as a **separate, slower revenue line**. It's never mixed in with recovering your table costs, so your numbers stay honest.

**06 · Recovery Breakdown**
One chart with the whole picture: on-site sales, follow-up, and network revenue stacked against the gap you're trying to close.

## 📊 The Summary Bar

Always pinned at the top while you scroll:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Total spend | Everything the event cost you |
| Gap to recover | Spend minus the organizer's contribution |
| Units needed | Sales still needed to break even |
| Net after event | Where you actually land |
| Profit margin | Net as a share of revenue |
| ROI on spend | Net as a return on what you put in |

## 🗂️ Multi-Event Tracking

- **Save multiple events.** Create, switch between, and delete event profiles.
- **Auto-saves as you type.** No save button needed.
- **Export / Import (.json).** Back up all your events or move them to another device.
- **Print / Save as PDF.** Get a clean summary to share or keep on file.
- **Toggle modules on or off.** Use only what fits the event.

> 🔒 **Your data stays on your device.** Everything is stored in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data erases it, so export a backup regularly.

---

## 📖 How to Use

1. **Before the event:** Fill in Module 01 (costs) and Module 02 (price and promised traffic). Know your break-even number going in.
2. **During the event:** Update Module 03 as the day goes on. If you're behind pace, adjust.
3. **After the event:** Log your leads in Modules 04 and 05 and follow up.
4. **Review:** Check the Recovery Breakdown, export your data, and use it to decide which events are worth booking again.
